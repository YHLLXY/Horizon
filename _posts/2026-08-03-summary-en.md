## [Hong Kong Launches China Government Bond Futures](https://36kr.com/newsflashes/3923229176016521?f=rss) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 3, 2026, Hong Kong Stock Exchange listed the first offshore China government bond \(CGB\) futures, a 5‑year contract that allows global investors to hedge onshore interest‑rate risk. The listing expands global access to China’s sovereign debt market, improves liquidity and pricing efficiency, and accelerates RMB internationalization by enabling offshore hedging and investment. The futures are traded and settled in Hong Kong using existing offshore accounts, bypassing QFII quota restrictions, and the product is priced in RMB, providing a direct link between onshore and offshore markets.

rss · 36氪 · Aug 3, 03:15

**Background**: China’s bond market is the world’s second largest, but until now, foreign investors could only trade onshore through the Qualified Foreign Institutional Investor \(QFII\) scheme. Bond futures are derivative contracts that allow investors to lock in future interest rates, reducing exposure to market volatility. Offshore bond futures provide a convenient, regulated venue for global investors to hedge and speculate without needing onshore access.
