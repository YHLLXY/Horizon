X has released its For You feed ranking algorithm as open source and launched tools that let users check if they have been shadowbanned. It may also pressure other social media to adopt similar accountability measures. The algorithm pulls roughly 1,500 candidate posts per session, scores them with Heavy Ranker, and ranks them for the For You feed; the new transparency tools expose the ranking impact on individual posts and accounts.

rss · TechCrunch · Aug 13, 16:00

The platform historically kept its ranking logic proprietary, leading to criticism over opaque shadowbanning. By open‑source the code, X aims to demystify how posts are surfaced and how moderation decisions are applied.

Recent debates over cyber sovereignty and the growing role of tech firms in national defense have set the stage for this policy change.
